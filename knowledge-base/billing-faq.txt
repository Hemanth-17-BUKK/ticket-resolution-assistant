Billing FAQ

Q: Why was I charged twice?
A: Duplicate charges can occur due to payment retries or banking delays. Our support team will verify the transactions and process a refund if necessary.

Q: How can I request an invoice?
A: Customers can request invoices by contacting support and providing their transaction ID.

Q: How long do refunds take?
A: Refunds usually take 5-7 business days.
